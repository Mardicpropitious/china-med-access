Travel & Accommodation for Medical Treatment in Shanghai

Visa support · Airport pickup · Hospital-area accommodation · Interpretation · Medical transport

Traveling to China for medical treatment involves more than a flight — visa documents, hospital-area accommodation, interpretation and safe arrival all need coordination. We treat travel and accommodation as part of the patient journey, with clear standards for safety.

Visa Support

Patients typically apply for a medical visa using a hospital invitation letter and medical appointment notice. We prepare these documents and guide you through the application. Visa approval, duration and required materials are decided by Chinese authorities and consulates — we can only assist with preparation, not guarantee approval.

Airport Pickup & Arrival

  • Before arrival we confirm your flight, companions, wheelchair needs and mobility requirements.
  • Our staff meet you with a name board, assist with luggage and transport, and reconfirm your first hospital appointment.
Important: ordinary pickup is not medical transport. Patients who are unstable, require oxygen, monitoring or stretcher transport must use qualified professional medical transport — we arrange this when clinically required.

In-Hospital Interpretation

Interpreters with medical competence accompany consultations and hospitalization. For complex procedures and informed-consent discussions, qualified medical interpreters are used — general-purpose translators are not substituted for surgical consent conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What visa do I need for medical treatment in China?

Patients typically apply for a medical visa using an invitation letter from the treating hospital plus a medical appointment notice. We assist with document preparation, but final visa decisions are made by Chinese consulates and authorities.

Can you arrange airport pickup and accommodation?

Yes. We coordinate airport pickup, hotel arrangements near the hospital (standard, serviced apartments, or accessible rooms), and daily transport coordination. Ordinary pickup is not medical transport; patients requiring oxygen, monitoring or stretcher transport are provided qualified medical transport.

Do you provide medical interpreters?

Yes. We arrange interpreters with medical competence for consultations and hospitalization. Complex informed-consent and surgical discussions are supported by qualified medical interpreters, not general-purpose translators.
