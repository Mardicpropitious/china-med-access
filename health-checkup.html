Health Check-up in Shanghai

Comprehensive screening · Cardiovascular risk assessment · Early cancer screening · Chronic disease management

Health check-ups in Shanghai are a practical entry point for international visitors: short treatment cycles, easy to organize, and available at accredited hospitals. Programs range from standard full-body screening to specialized cardiovascular risk assessment and early cancer screening.

Common Check-up Components

  • Full-body screening — blood panels, imaging and organ function tests.
  • Cardiovascular risk assessment — blood pressure, lipid profile, cardiac imaging and risk scoring.
  • Early cancer screening — tumor markers and imaging appropriate to age and risk factors.
  • Chronic disease evaluation — diabetes, thyroid and metabolic checks.
  • Physician consultation — a doctor explains findings and recommends follow-up.

How Booking Works

  1. Choose a program — we share available hospital check-up packages with prices.
  2. Hospital confirmation — the hospital confirms the program, price and appointment.
  3. Visit & results — you attend the check-up (with interpretation); results are delivered with a physician consultation.
  4. Follow-up — a report in English (and your language where needed) for your home physician.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Shanghai health check-up include?

Programs commonly include full-body screening (blood tests, imaging), cardiovascular risk assessment, early cancer screening and chronic disease evaluation. The exact panel is confirmed by the hospital before you book.

How long does a health check-up take?

Most comprehensive check-up programs take one to two days, with results typically delivered shortly after, followed by a physician consultation to explain findings. Exact timelines are confirmed by the hospital.

Do I need a referral to book a health check-up?

No referral is needed. You can book a health check-up program directly through our coordination service; the hospital confirms the program and price before your visit.
