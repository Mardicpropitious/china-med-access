How It Works — Your Medical Journey in China

ChinaMed Access · Updated November 7, 2026

Seeking medical treatment in China as an international patient follows a structured process: submit your case, get an independent physician assessment, receive a formal treatment plan and cost estimate, arrange travel, receive treatment in Shanghai, and continue follow-up after returning home.

Every patient is assigned one dedicated case manager who stays with you from the first consultation through post-discharge follow-up. You deal with one team, not ten different contacts.

The 10-Step Patient Journey

1

Contact & Initial Consultation

Tell us about the condition, your country, treatment history and questions. We confirm whether your situation falls within services we can coordinate.

2

Medical Record Collection

Submit reports, imaging, pathology, surgical records and medication history. With your authorization, we organize and translate them into the format hospitals require.

3

Hospital & Specialist Matching

Based on your condition, we recommend appropriate accredited hospitals and departments. The treating physician independently evaluates your case.

4

Treatment Plan & Cost Estimate

The hospital provides a formal treatment proposal, cost estimate, expected duration, risks and rehabilitation plan. You decide whether to proceed.

5

Appointment & Visa Support

We coordinate the hospital appointment and prepare visa documentation (invitation letter, medical appointment notice). Final visa decisions rest with Chinese authorities.

6

Travel & Arrival

We help with flights, hotel arrangements near the hospital, airport pickup and initial orientation — including wheelchair or special-needs arrangements if required.

7

Treatment in Shanghai

Hospitals deliver medical care. Our team provides in-hospital interpretation, document assistance, daily coordination and communication with your family.

8

Discharge & Documentation

We prepare your discharge summary, pathology, imaging, medication list, wound care instructions and follow-up schedule — in English and, where needed, your language.

9

Return Travel

The hospital assesses whether you are fit to fly. We coordinate safe return arrangements, including medical transport if clinically required.

10

Follow-up at Home

We connect you with your local physician, arrange remote consultations, and support any necessary return visits to China.

Key Principles

  • Medical decisions belong to doctors: Whether you are suitable for treatment, fit to fly, or ready for discharge is decided by the treating hospital — never by our coordination staff.
  • Transparent fees: Hospital medical fees are paid directly to the hospital. Our coordination service fees are itemized and separate.
  • Your data is protected: Medical records are collected with your authorization, stored securely, and shared only with the hospitals and physicians involved in your care.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the case review take?

After you submit complete medical records, our coordination team checks the documents and forwards them to an appropriate hospital department. The treating physician independently assesses whether the case is suitable and provides a response, typically within a few business days depending on complexity.

Do I need to pay before the hospital assessment?

No. Initial case coordination and physician assessment do not require payment to ChinaMed Access. Medical fees are paid directly to the hospital after a formal treatment plan and cost estimate are provided.

Can family members accompany me?

Yes. Family members can accompany patients and we can help arrange accommodation, translation and daily coordination for companions as well.
