Request a Medical Case Review

Free initial coordination assessment · No payment required for the first review

Medical case review is the first step for international patients considering treatment in China. Submit your medical records and basic information; our coordination team checks the documents, matches appropriate hospitals and specialists, and the treating physician independently assesses your case.

What Happens After You Submit

  1. Document check — our team reviews your records and contacts you if anything is missing.
  2. Hospital matching — we recommend appropriate accredited hospitals and departments.
  3. Physician assessment — the treating specialist evaluates whether your case is suitable.
  4. Response & next steps — you receive the hospital's feedback, a treatment proposal and cost estimate if applicable.

Case Review Form

Your information is collected with your authorization, stored securely, and shared only with the hospitals and physicians involved in your case assessment.

Compliance note: Submitting this form does not create a doctor–patient relationship and does not guarantee treatment or clinical trial participation. Medical suitability is determined by qualified physicians at accredited hospitals. This form is a coordination request, not a medical advertisement.

Medical disclaimer: This website is informational. ChinaMed Access is a coordination service, not a medical institution. In case of emergency, contact your local emergency services.
