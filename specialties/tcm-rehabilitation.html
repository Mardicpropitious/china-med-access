Traditional Chinese Medicine & Rehabilitation in Shanghai

Acupuncture · Tuina · Herbal medicine · Chronic pain management · Post-operative rehabilitation

Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) is a comprehensive medical system practiced in China by licensed practitioners at accredited TCM hospitals. For international patients, it offers distinctive options — from acupuncture and Tuina (therapeutic massage) to herbal medicine, dietary therapy and mind-body practices — often combined with rehabilitation after surgery.

Services We Coordinate

  • Acupuncture — for pain management and symptom support.
  • Tuina & manual therapy — therapeutic massage and musculoskeletal treatment.
  • Herbal medicine — individualized formulas prescribed by licensed TCM physicians.
  • Chronic pain management — integrative approaches for persistent pain.
  • Post-operative rehabilitation — TCM-assisted recovery programs.
  • Integrative care — combining TCM with conventional treatment where clinically appropriate.

Programs & Duration

We can design 5–14 day TCM wellness and rehabilitation itineraries in Shanghai, combining daily treatments with rest and cultural recovery. Programs are customized after a physician assessment of your condition and goals.

Important Boundaries

  • TCM is not a substitute for evidence-based cancer treatments, surgery, chemotherapy or radiotherapy — it may support symptom management and rehabilitation, never replace proven care.
  • All treatments are delivered by qualified practitioners based on individual assessment.
  • Bringing herbal products home is subject to destination-country customs and pharmaceutical import rules — check these before purchase; we cannot guarantee herbal products may be freely carried across borders.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is traditional Chinese medicine (TCM)?

Traditional Chinese medicine is a comprehensive medical system that includes acupuncture, Tuina (massage), herbal medicine, dietary therapy and mind-body practices, delivered in China by licensed practitioners at accredited TCM hospitals.

Is TCM suitable as a complement to cancer treatment?

TCM may support symptom management and rehabilitation, but it is not a substitute for evidence-based cancer treatments such as surgery, chemotherapy or radiotherapy. All treatments are delivered by qualified practitioners based on individual assessment.

Can I bring herbal medicine back to my country?

Bringing herbal medicine across borders is subject to the customs and pharmaceutical import rules of your destination country. We advise you to check these rules before purchase; we cannot guarantee that herbal products may be freely carried home.
