Internal Medicine in China

Cardiology · Endocrinology · Gastroenterology · Neurology · Multidisciplinary consultation

Internal medicine consultation in China gives international patients access to experienced specialists at leading Shanghai hospitals — for evaluation, treatment planning and chronic disease management across cardiology, endocrinology, gastroenterology, immunology, neurology and respiratory medicine.

Specialties Commonly Coordinated

  • Cardiology — risk assessment, hypertension, arrhythmia and coronary evaluation.
  • Endocrinology — diabetes, thyroid disorders and metabolic disease management.
  • Gastroenterology — digestive disorders, liver disease and endoscopic evaluation.
  • Neurology — headache, movement disorders and cerebrovascular risk assessment.
  • Immunology & rheumatology — autoimmune and chronic inflammatory conditions.
  • Chronic disease management — coordinated long-term care programs.

Shanghai Specialist Medical Consultation Program

Our structured consultation program works like this:

  1. Document pre-review: you submit medical records; we prepare and translate them for the hospital.
  2. Department matching: we recommend an appropriate hospital department for your condition.
  3. Outpatient consultation: you meet the specialist in person (with interpretation); the hospital may arrange a multidisciplinary consultation when needed.
  4. Treatment plan: the physician provides recommendations, prescriptions and follow-up schedule.

Remote document pre-review is not a formal diagnosis. Cross-border internet healthcare, where applicable, is subject to hospital rules and applicable regulations — a definitive evaluation is conducted in person.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I see a specialist in Shanghai for a second opinion?

Yes. Our Shanghai Specialist Medical Consultation Program coordinates outpatient consultations with appropriate hospital departments — including multidisciplinary consultations when needed. Remote document pre-review is not a formal diagnosis; a formal evaluation happens during the in-person consultation.

Which internal medicine specialties are available?

Commonly coordinated specialties include cardiology, endocrinology, gastroenterology, immunology, neurology, respiratory medicine and chronic disease management, at accredited Shanghai hospitals.

Is remote consultation available for internal medicine?

Remote document pre-review and, where applicable, authorized telemedicine arrangements can be coordinated, subject to the hospital's rules and applicable regulations on cross-border internet healthcare. Final evaluation is typically conducted in person.
