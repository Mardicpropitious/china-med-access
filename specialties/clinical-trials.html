Clinical Trial Information in China

Information coordination for qualified clinical research studies

Clinical trials in China — formally, clinical research studies conducted at accredited research institutions — offer some patients access to investigational treatments under rigorous ethical and regulatory oversight. ChinaMed Access provides information coordination: we help patients understand what studies exist, what screening involves, and how logistics work. We do not enroll patients, do not sell trial slots, and do not charge fees tied to enrollment or outcomes.

Clinical trial eligibility is decided exclusively by the research institution — based on the study protocol, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and ethics review requirements. We coordinate information, language support and logistics only.

What We Coordinate

  • Study information: helping you identify publicly registered studies and research institutions relevant to your condition and treatment history.
  • Record preparation: organizing and translating medical records with your authorization.
  • Submission: forwarding your information to the research institution, which independently assesses eligibility.
  • Language & logistics: supporting communication, travel and stay arrangements if you are invited to participate.

Important Boundaries

  • We do not promise that you can enter any clinical trial.
  • We do not collect commissions conditional on enrollment.
  • We do not advertise trials as "free treatment" or guarantee efficacy.
  • Informed consent, screening, enrollment and all medical management during a study are handled by the research institution under applicable regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can international patients join clinical trials in China?

Eligibility for clinical trials is determined exclusively by the research institution based on the study protocol, inclusion and exclusion criteria, and ethics review requirements. ChinaMed Access coordinates information and logistics only; we do not enroll patients or guarantee participation.

Do patients pay for clinical trial participation?

Research-related procedures are managed under the study's rules and applicable regulations; patients should not be charged for research costs that the study is required to bear. Any coordination service fees are separate, itemized, and never tied to screening, enrollment, or study outcomes.

How is informed consent handled for foreign patients?

Informed consent is obtained by the research institution's authorized staff, with appropriate language support, in accordance with the study protocol and ethics review requirements. We support language and logistics but do not replace the formal consent process.
