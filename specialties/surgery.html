Surgery & Orthopedics in China

Joint replacement · Spine · Sports medicine · Neurosurgery · Minimally invasive surgery

Orthopedic and surgical treatment in China attracts international patients for procedures including hip and knee replacement, spine surgery, sports medicine and complex general surgery. Chinese teaching hospitals perform high volumes of these procedures, and many patients find competitive pricing compared with Western centers.

Common Procedures Coordinated

  • Hip & knee replacement — total joint arthroplasty with experienced surgical teams.
  • Spine surgery — decompression, fusion and deformity correction.
  • Sports medicine & arthroscopy — ligament reconstruction, meniscus and cartilage procedures.
  • Neurosurgery — at specialized centers (e.g., Huashan Hospital's renowned neurosurgery department).
  • Minimally invasive & robotic-assisted surgery — for abdominal, thoracic and urologic conditions.
  • Orthopedic rehabilitation — post-operative recovery programs.

Before You Travel: What to Prepare

  1. Imaging and clinical records: X-rays, CT/MRI, prior operation notes and medication history.
  2. Physician assessment: the hospital confirms surgical indication, approach and expected outcomes.
  3. Written cost estimate: hospital fees (surgery, implant, hospital stay) are confirmed in writing before you travel.
  4. Post-surgery planning: rehabilitation schedule, expected length of stay, and fitness-to-fly assessment by the hospital.

Post-Surgery & Rehabilitation

Recovery planning is part of the service: we coordinate rehabilitation arrangements, discharge documentation (surgical records, pathology, medication and wound care instructions), and follow-up with your local orthopedic surgeon after you return home. For patients at risk of complications — such as deep vein thrombosis or infection — the hospital will define appropriate precautions, and medical transport can be arranged when clinically required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is joint replacement surgery available to international patients in China?

Yes. Hip and knee replacement, spine surgery and sports medicine are among the most common procedures arranged for international patients at Shanghai hospitals, subject to physician assessment of the individual case.

How long is the hospital stay for joint replacement in China?

Hospital stay depends on the procedure and the patient's condition, and is determined by the treating hospital. Typical stays range from several days to one to two weeks, followed by rehabilitation planning. Your hospital will provide a written plan before travel.

Can I fly home after surgery?

Whether a patient is fit to fly is decided by the treating hospital, considering risks such as thrombosis, infection and post-operative complications. For patients with higher risk, medical transport or extended stay may be recommended.
