About ChinaMed Access

International patient coordination platform · Shanghai, China

ChinaMed Access is an international patient coordination platform. We connect overseas patients with accredited hospitals and qualified specialists in Shanghai, China — and support them through the entire journey: case review, appointments, translation, travel, treatment coordination and follow-up care.

Our Model

  • Overseas patient acquisition — digital channels and partner networks in target countries help patients learn about treatment options in China.
  • Shanghai medical resources — a curated network of accredited hospitals and departments with international patient service capability.
  • Full-journey patient service — one dedicated case manager per patient, from first consultation to post-discharge follow-up.
  • Separate, transparent fees — hospital medical fees are paid directly to the hospital; our coordination service fees are itemized separately.

Our Principles

  1. Medical decisions belong to physicians. Whether a case is suitable, when a patient can fly, and when discharge is safe are decided by treating hospitals — never by coordination staff.
  2. No commission on treatment. We do not earn from hospital medical fees, and we do not make treatment promises on behalf of doctors.
  3. Patient data protection. Records are collected with authorization, stored securely, and shared only with the hospitals and physicians involved in care.
  4. Compliance first. We operate within China's medical advertising and cross-border data rules; we are a coordination service, not a medical institution.

What We Are Not

  • We are not a hospital and do not provide diagnosis or treatment.
  • We do not guarantee treatment outcomes, cure rates, or clinical trial enrollment.
  • We do not publish medical advertisements on behalf of hospitals.

Compliance note: Per China's Medical Advertisement Recognition Guidelines (2025), entities other than licensed medical institutions may not publish medical advertisements. ChinaMed Access provides coordination services, publishes objective hospital information with authorization, and submits patient needs to hospitals for independent medical evaluation.

Medical disclaimer: This website provides general information for educational purposes. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider. In case of emergency, contact local emergency services.
