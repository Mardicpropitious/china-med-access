From Jakarta to Shanghai: Liver Cancer Surgery

Anonymized case · Hepatobiliary surgery · Coordination timeline: ~3 weeks

A 52-year-old patient from Indonesia was diagnosed with early-stage hepatocellular carcinoma. With limited local surgical options and long waiting times, the family contacted us after seeing information about liver surgery programs in Shanghai.

  • Week 1: Medical records (CT imaging, pathology, blood work) submitted; our team organized translation and forwarded them to a hepatobiliary department at a Fudan-affiliated cancer center.
  • Week 2: The treating physician confirmed surgical suitability; a written plan and cost estimate were provided. The family requested a remote second opinion before deciding.
  • Week 3: Visa documents prepared; the patient traveled with a family member; airport pickup and hotel near the hospital arranged.
  • Treatment: Laparoscopic liver resection performed; hospital stay with in-hospital interpretation; discharge package with pathology, imaging and medication list in English.
  • Follow-up: Remote follow-up with the patient's local oncologist, including imaging review at 3 months.

Knee Replacement for a Malaysian Patient

Anonymized case · Orthopedic surgery · Coordination timeline: ~2 weeks

A 61-year-old patient from Malaysia with advanced knee osteoarthritis had been considering joint replacement for two years. Concerned about cost and waiting times at home, the patient compared options and chose a Shanghai orthopedic program.

  • Assessment: X-ray and MRI reviewed by a joint surgery specialist at a Shanghai orthopedic center; the patient was confirmed suitable for total knee replacement.
  • Planning: Written cost estimate including implant options; rehabilitation plan and expected length of stay explained in advance.
  • Treatment: Total knee replacement with a two-week rehabilitation program; daily coordination by a dedicated case manager.
  • Return: Fitness-to-fly confirmed by the hospital; discharge documents, home exercise program, and remote follow-up at 6 weeks with a local physiotherapist.

Chronic Pain: A 10-Day TCM Program from Vietnam

Anonymized case · TCM & rehabilitation · Coordination timeline: ~1 week + 10-day program

A 45-year-old patient from Vietnam with chronic lower-back pain had tried physiotherapy at home without sustained improvement. Interested in traditional Chinese medicine, the patient enrolled in a structured acupuncture and Tuina program.

  • Pre-travel: Medical history and prior imaging reviewed by a TCM physician at a Shanghai university TCM hospital.
  • Program: Daily acupuncture sessions, Tuina therapy, and dietary guidance over 10 days; interpretation and daily coordination included.
  • Outcome: The patient received a written summary with home-care recommendations and returned for a follow-up program six months later.

What These Stories Show

  • Every journey is coordinated by one dedicated case manager.
  • Physicians decide suitability — before travel, in writing.
  • Transparent costs — hospital fees and coordination fees are separate.
  • Follow-up continues after return — with the patient's local physicians.
